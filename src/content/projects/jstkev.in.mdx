Welcome to version **three** of my personal website. Yeah, I know. **Three** completely different looks, and two earlier versions I'd rather not show you. Two more attempts than anyone asked for, and roughly two more than I'm proud of.

The embarrassing part isn't that I rebuilt it — it's that I did it twice before this one. New layout, new look, same me squinting at it and going *nah — scrap it, from the top*. Three different designs to say the same handful of things.

Third time, I finally left it alone long enough to finish. The repo's linked above if you want to pick through it.

Honestly the part I'm happiest with is the torii on the landing page. It's a little [three.js](https://threejs.org/) watercolor gate — move your mouse around and it tilts with you, click it and the water ripples. Mostly just an excuse to finally learn three.js, tbh.

Fun fact though: the torii isn't new. I first made it back in 2022, when I was deep in a VFX phase, and it ended up as a [Wallpaper Engine wallpaper](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2805940658) you can still download. Funny how it ended up back here, four years later (AOT level foreshadowing). 

Anyway. Three designs later, this is the one that stuck (I hope). If you're here, go poke around — and I wouldn't rush through the nav if I were you.
